## What changed with my money?

Stoke Money brings the details of everyday finances together. The app is designed to show what drove a change in spending, what has already cleared, and which recurring charges are ahead. A paper-inspired register and an illustrated companion give the numbers some personality.

## Try the interactive preview

Open [the sample ledger](https://www.stokemoney.com/) and punch a row to meet Stoke. The preview uses fictional transactions and is open now. You can also explore [free money tools](https://www.stokemoney.com/tools) for spending, saving, and financial independence while the app is in development.
